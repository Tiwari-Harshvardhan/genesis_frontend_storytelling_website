3.8 billion years ago, in the scalding mineral broth of Earth's nascent oceans, the first stirrings of life emerged — not with a thunderclap, but with a whisper. Self-replicating molecules, impossibly fragile, impossibly persistent, began the longest experiment in cosmic history.
